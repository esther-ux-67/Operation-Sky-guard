Operation Sky Guard

Bombers are overhead and enemy soldiers are closing in on foot. Lead stranded civilians into the bunker before they are lost.

How to play

  • Walk up to a civilian and they follow you. Each follower slows you by 3% (up to 60%).
  • Enter the glowing bunker zone to drop off your whole group. Rescue the quota shown at the top right before the raid timer ends. It starts at 7 and grows by 2 each level up to 21.
  • Red rings show where bombs will land. Get out before they fill.
  • Red soldiers hunt civilians and hurt you on contact. Shoot them down.
  • Rubble blocks you and your shots but shields you from blasts. Mud halves your speed.
  • You have 100 HP and the bunker has 500. Lose either and the raid fails.

Controls

MoveWASD, arrow keys, or hold on touch screens
ShootSpace (hold to keep firing, aims at the nearest soldier)
DashShift (short burst, 2 second cooldown)
PauseP (then M for the menu)
